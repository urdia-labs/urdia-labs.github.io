Privacy Policy

Effective date: October 6, 2026

1. Scope

This Privacy Policy explains how URDIA Labs handles information in connection with the URDIA web platform and this public website.

2. Public website

This website does not provide advertising or embedded tracking tools. The site is hosted through GitHub Pages, and GitHub may process technical request data under its own privacy practices.

3. Web application

URDIA is designed to process project data and generated media in accordance with the features and services enabled by the user. Data sent to connected third-party services is handled under the policies of those services.

4. TikTok connections

When a user explicitly connects TikTok, URDIA may process the platform account identifier, basic profile information, authorization codes and tokens required to maintain the authorized connection. Client secrets and refresh tokens must remain server-side and are not intended for public disclosure.

5. Publishing

When a user initiates publication, URDIA sends the content and required metadata to the authorized third-party platform. URDIA does not claim ownership of that content. The third party processes the submission under its own terms and privacy policy.

6. Data minimization

URDIA requests only the permissions needed for the configured integration. The application is designed not to sell personal information and not to use platform credentials for advertising.

7. Retention and deletion

Retention depends on the enabled URDIA features and connected services. Users may revoke third-party authorization through the relevant platform and may request deletion of information handled by URDIA where applicable.

8. Third-party services

Third-party APIs, hosting providers and AI/media providers have separate policies and terms. URDIA is not responsible for data practices outside its own application and website.

9. Contact

Questions about this policy can be directed to the URDIA Labs project through GitHub.

10. Changes

This policy may be updated when the product, integrations or applicable requirements change. The effective date above identifies the current version published on this site.
